The first release of the unofficial native PC port of 007: Agent Under Fire (original Xbox): the game's own code statically
recompiled into Windows programs, not an emulator.

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the European (PAL) Xbox release: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder
with its files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## Getting started

1. Download **AgentUnderFireLauncher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own.
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder), accept Microsoft's license for its C++ libraries, press **Build**. The first build takes
   a while: the game is five programs.
4. Press **Play**. **PC Settings** is in the main menu and **RECOMP** is a tab of the pause screen; **F1** opens the full settings
   window at any time.

## What's in it

- **On foot, driving and mounted guns**, in English, French and German, with the switch between the game's programs done for you.
- **Widescreen and ultrawide**: the 3D view widened, the menus and HUD kept in proportion.
- **High frame rates**: on foot the game follows the frame rate cap, up to 240 or unlimited, with its speed tied to real time.
  Driving and mounted guns run at 60 game steps a second and above a cap of 60 draw frames in between.
- **Mouse and keyboard**: direct mouse look and gun aim, a pointer in the menus, rebindable keys, and prompts that show your keys.
- **Graphics**: render scale up to 200%, MSAA, FXAA, SMAA, smooth alpha edges, sharpening, texture filtering, a sharp scaling
  filter, enhanced textures, sharp text and HUD, and a field of view from 50 to 150%.
- **Achievements**, optional auto-aim, skip intro movies, and F12 screenshots.

## Good to know

- Driving missions and mounted guns play about a fifth faster than the PAL console, because they run at 60 game steps a second
  instead of 50. Turning off High Frame Rate returns them to 50, and on foot to the console's 30.
- Slow motion in driving cut scenes is held to half speed, so it is not down to a quarter of the frames.
- Above a cap of 60, in driving missions and on mounted guns, turn **Vertical Sync off** for the most frames. Some wall textures
  can still flicker slightly there; a cap of 60 draws no frames in between.
- **Enhanced Textures** can cause a brief hitch as new textures appear. It is off by default.
- Multiplayer has not been tried, and not every later mission has been played through. Please report what you find.
